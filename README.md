# AC Digitals Showcase

Create a premium, high-converting international website for my web design and digital services business called **AC DIGITALS**.

The website should feel like a **luxury international digital agency**, not a generic template or beginner freelancer website. The goal is to attract customers from **South Africa and internationally**, generate qualified leads, showcase my web design services, establish trust, and encourage visitors to contact me for a website quotation.

## BRAND

Business name:
**AC DIGITALS**

Tagline:
**Premium Websites. Powerful First Impressions.**

Business location:
**Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa**

Position the company as a **professional website design business serving clients locally and internationally**.

Use the uploaded AC DIGITALS logo prominently and professionally throughout the website. Do not redesign or distort the logo. Build the website's visual identity around the logo.

## DESIGN DIRECTION

Create an exceptionally polished, modern and luxurious visual experience.

Style:

* Luxury technology agency
* Premium digital studio
* Sophisticated
* Minimal but visually impressive
* High-end typography
* Elegant spacing
* Premium glassmorphism elements where appropriate
* Subtle gradients
* Beautiful dark/light contrast
* Smooth animations
* Modern cards
* Soft shadows
* Elegant borders
* Subtle glowing accents
* Interactive hover effects
* Smooth scrolling
* Micro-interactions
* Professional transitions

The design should communicate:

**"This is a serious professional web design company that can build a website worthy of my business."**

Avoid:

* Cheap-looking templates
* Excessive bright colors
* Clutter
* Cartoon graphics
* Generic stock-template appearance
* Amateur-looking layouts
* Excessive animations that hurt performance

Use a premium dark aesthetic as the primary design direction, with sophisticated accent colors inspired by the AC DIGITALS logo.

Make the site fully responsive and beautiful on:

* Desktop
* Laptop
* Tablet
* Mobile

Mobile experience must be exceptionally polished.
